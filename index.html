Behind the Palette

Meet Amalie Baker

Operating from her quiet sanctuary in Saint Paul, Minnesota, Amalie translates visual aesthetics into profound, resonant emotion. She views digital portraiture not merely as layout and color mapping, but as an intimate channel of healing, reflection, and quiet grace.

Her signature style features complex, mosaic-like brushstrokes. She layers rich neon-vibrancy with quiet, grounding pastels to reflect the beautifully messy contradictions of human nature. This fusion of heavy texture and digital painting brings a tangible, high-definition presence to any custom print or screen.
